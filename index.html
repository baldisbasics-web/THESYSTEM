Quests
An endless path of bodyweight and dumbbell workouts (up to 12kg), each one harder than the last. Only your current quest is shown — the next one stays hidden until you clear this one. Cleared quests move into Completed Quests below, where you can still redo them anytime for half the reward.
Settings
Adjust today's targets manually if the auto-scaled numbers don't fit you.
Reward Shop
Clearing an objective earns 10 PTS. Clearing the full Daily Quest earns a 40 PTS bonus (halved during a Penalty). Spend points to stock up on treats, then use them from your Inventory whenever you want. Prices creep up as your level rises — shown prices always reflect your current level.
